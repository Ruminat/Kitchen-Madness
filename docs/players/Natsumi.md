# Natsumi

> A young dreamer chasing the science of flavor — the would-be Einstein of cooking.

## Story

Natsumi is a Japanese girl with an enormous dream: to become the greatest sushi
maker in all of Japan. She traveled to Tokyo to learn from the very best, but her
ambition quickly outgrew a single craft. Now she wants to explore every cuisine
there is, to understand cooking down to its foundations — and then to reinvent food
itself. Her dream isn't just to master recipes; it's to become the Einstein of the
kitchen, rewriting the rules of what a meal can be.

Mechanically, she's the balanced all-rounder. Nothing about her is extreme, but
everything about her is good — a clean, flexible starting point that can grow toward
almost any build.

## Stats

Base stats (shared by all players): **move speed 30**, **armor 0**.

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Health | 80 HP |
| Damage | +12% |
| Attack speed | +12% |
| Crit chance | +12% |
| Luck | +6% |
| Evasion | 0% |

## Playstyle

- **Fantasy:** the versatile experimenter — a little bit of everything, weak at nothing.
- **Strengths:** balanced positive modifiers across damage, attack speed, and crit
  make almost every weapon and upgrade path viable.
- **Weaknesses:** the lowest health of the three, so positioning matters; she has no
  single standout stat to carry a lopsided build.
- **Build toward:** whatever the run offers — she flexes into crit, raw damage, or
  attack speed equally well. Shore up survivability early to offset the thin HP pool.
