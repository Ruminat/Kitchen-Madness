# The Newbie

> The rookie who hasn't found his style yet — clumsy hands, but a spark of something greater.

## Story

Nobody knows his name. He's just a rookie stumbling around the kitchen, trying to
find his own way of cooking. He's awkward, he burns half of what he touches, and he
drops more than he plates — but underneath the fumbling there's a huge, unproven
potential. Some hidden power is waiting to be unleashed; he just hasn't figured out
how to reach it yet.

He's the character for players who want to grow *into* their strength. He starts
fragile and hits a little soft, but his blazing attack speed, sky-high crit chance,
and good fortune mean that once the run snowballs, he snowballs harder than anyone.

## Stats

Base stats (shared by all players): **move speed 30**, **armor 0**.

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Health | 90 HP |
| Damage | −12% |
| Attack speed | +18% |
| Crit chance | +24% |
| Luck | +12% |
| Evasion | 0% |

## Playstyle

- **Fantasy:** the underdog who blooms late — weak early, terrifying once built.
- **Strengths:** highest attack speed and crit chance in the starting roster, plus
  strong luck for more drops and duplicate offers to double down on a build.
- **Weaknesses:** low health and a damage penalty make the opening minutes risky.
- **Build toward:** flat damage, crit damage, and survivability (HP / armor / evasion)
  to convert all those extra crits and swings into a runaway lead.
