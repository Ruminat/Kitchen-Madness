# Mr. Barret

> The failed businessman who cooks because it's the one thing he's actually good at.

## Story

Mr. Barret never wanted to be a cook. He dreamed of business — deals, ledgers, an
empire of his own. He just happened to be terrible at all of it. Every venture
collapsed, and eventually he fell back on the only thing his hands truly knew:
cooking. So now he ties on the apron over a pressed suit, telling himself that one
day he'll open his own restaurant and finally become the businessman he always
wanted to be.

Until then, he cooks — heavily, reliably, and with the stubbornness of a man who
refuses to fail twice. He's the sturdy, hard-hitting pick: slow and steady, built to
take punishment and answer it with big, deliberate blows.

## Playstyle

- **Fantasy:** the dependable heavyweight — high health, high damage, no gimmicks.
- **Strengths:** the biggest health pool and the hardest hits of the starting roster;
  forgiving for new players and a strong anchor for slow, powerful weapons.
- **Weaknesses:** no attack-speed, crit, or luck edge — his damage comes per hit,
  not per second, so he can feel sluggish against fast swarms.
- **Build toward:** attack speed and area to fix his pace, or lean further into raw
  damage and armor to become an unstoppable wall.
